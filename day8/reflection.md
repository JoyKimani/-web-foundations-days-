The most difficult concept in the course was preventing two customers from buying the same seat during a sudden sale. A cached seat map can become stale immediately, so correctness cannot depend on what the browser displays. I worked through the race by modeling each event seat as a database row, locking the requested rows inside a transaction, and making the hold all-or-nothing. A uniqueness constraint on active or paid orders provides a second safeguard. Thinking through payment timeouts and expired holds helped me see why idempotency and reconciliation matter as much as the initial transaction.
