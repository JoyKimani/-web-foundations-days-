About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight web application designed to help you capture your thoughts and ideas before they disappear. It gives you a clean, distraction-free workspace to quickly organize daily tasks and notes.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note into the "New note" input box.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button or press Enter to add it to your list.
  3. Review your active notes counter and track tasks as you finish them.

Features

  • Fast and distraction-free note creation
  • Real-time counter displaying your total notes
  • Accessible semantic HTML5 structure
  • Full keyboard navigation support

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Submit a new note
